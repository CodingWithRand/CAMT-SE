My hobbies

Digital Art (Drawing for my dear friend 😽)

I have a friend who I know from online, and we have been friends for around 3 years now. I like to draw digital art for her as a gift on special occasions like her birthday or Christmas. At that time, I draw using my finger on my phone or using mouse on my laptop. That seems hard to you right? Not really, but it did take a very long time to finish it. If you like someone to some extent, you will do anything for them, whatever it takes, right? That same goes to me. Well, she's really happy when I do something for her, and that makes me happy too. I don't know if I can call it a hobby, but I like to do it, so I will put it here.
